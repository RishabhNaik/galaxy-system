# 01 - Company Overview & Vision

## Galaxy System: Overview
Galaxy System is a Bengaluru-based assistive mobility company dedicated to improving the independence and quality of life for persons with disabilities. The organization specializes in adaptive vehicle modifications, prosthetic limbs, orthotic devices, and comprehensive mobility solutions.

Galaxy System focuses on creating **customized solutions** rather than promoting universal products. Each recommendation is based on the individual's medical condition, functional ability, and long-term mobility goals. The company serves individuals with a wide range of conditions including post-polio residual paralysis, spinal cord injuries, limb amputations, cerebral palsy, muscular disorders, and congenital limb differences.

The company is an RTO-approved vehicle modification provider, ensuring all adaptations comply with legal and safety standards. Galaxy System assists customers with understanding government benefits, including road tax exemptions and insurance concessions.

## The Founder's Story
Established in **1994**, Galaxy System initially operated in the field of **computers and computer peripherals** (not the broader IT sector). In 2008, the founder — a **right lower-limb polio survivor** — purchased a **Hyundai Verna** and could not find a technically sound, RTO-compliant adaptive driving solution despite consulting multiple workshops.

Recognizing this as a widespread unmet need, he transitioned Galaxy System into an adaptive vehicle modification provider in **2009**. In **2011**, the founder underwent specialized training with **Kivi Mobility Freedom SRL, Italy** — one of the world's leading manufacturers of adaptive driving equipment. His sustained work was recognised with the **Karma Veera Puraskara (2014)** for social contribution and an **Honorary Doctorate in Social Service**. The company has served approximately **3,000–4,000 adaptive vehicle customers** over the years.

## Vision & Mission
**Vision:** To become India's most trusted provider of adaptive mobility solutions — creating a society where physical disability is never a barrier to mobility, employment, or independent living.

**Mission:** To restore independence through customized, safe, and legally compliant engineering solutions. Galaxy System ensures every recommendation is rooted in medical assessment and that customers receive education on adaptive technologies, government benefits, rehabilitation, and long-term maintenance.

## Core Philosophy & Values
- **Every Person is Different:** Solutions begin with detailed assessment, not predefined products.
- **Customisation Over Standardisation:** Prosthetics, orthotics, and adaptive driving systems are tailored to the individual's anatomy, ability, and daily requirements.
- **Safety Before Convenience:** Safety and legal compliance are the highest priorities in every engineering decision.
- **Independence with Dignity:** Mobility enables education, employment, healthcare, and social participation.
- **Engineering Meets Rehabilitation:** A multidisciplinary approach ensures technology complements physical abilities rather than forcing adaptation.
- **Core Values:** Empathy, Integrity, Innovation, Quality, Compliance, and Social Impact.
