## Glossary of Terms
- **Adaptive Driving:** Operating a vehicle using specialized controls designed for persons with disabilities.
- **AFO (Ankle Foot Orthosis):** Extends from below the knee to the foot; controls ankle position and improves gait.
- **Alignment:** Positioning of prosthetic/orthotic components for optimal comfort and function.
- **Dynamic Alignment:** Fine adjustment of a prosthesis during active walking to improve gait symmetry and comfort.
- **Endoskeletal Prosthesis:** A prosthesis with an internal structural frame (pylon) covered by a cosmetic foam cover. Modular and adjustable.
- **Exoskeletal Prosthesis:** A prosthesis with a rigid outer shell providing both structure and appearance.
- **FO (Foot Orthosis):** A custom insole or foot support to improve foot alignment and pressure distribution.
- **Gait Analysis:** Clinical evaluation of walking patterns to guide rehabilitation and device design.
- **KAFO (Knee-Ankle-Foot Orthosis):** Extends from thigh to foot; used for severe lower-limb weakness or paralysis.
- **KO (Knee Orthosis):** A brace supporting or stabilizing the knee joint.
- **Liner:** A soft cushioning sleeve (silicone/gel) that protects the residual limb inside a prosthetic socket.
- **Myoelectric Prosthesis:** A powered prosthesis controlled by electrical signals from residual limb muscles (EMG).
- **Orthosis:** An externally applied device to support, stabilize, correct, or improve the function of a body part (limb or spine). The limb is present.
- **Post-Polio Residual Paralysis (PPRP):** Weakness or paralysis in limbs affected by polio, persisting after the acute phase.
- **Prosthesis:** An artificial device replacing a missing limb or body part.
- **Push-Pull Hand Control:** An adaptive driving device where the driver pushes to brake and pulls to accelerate, entirely bypassing foot pedals.
- **Rehabilitation:** The process of restoring or improving functional ability after injury, illness, or disability.
- **Residual Limb (Stump):** The remaining portion of a limb following amputation.
- **RTO (Regional Transport Office):** Government authority responsible for vehicle registration, driving licences, and approval of vehicle modifications.
- **Socket:** The component of a prosthesis that fits over and connects to the residual limb — the most critical element for fit and comfort.
- **Suspension System:** The mechanism securing a prosthesis to the body (e.g., suction, pin-lock, vacuum, sleeve).
- **Transfemoral Prosthesis:** An above-knee prosthesis; includes a prosthetic knee mechanism.
- **Transtibial Prosthesis:** A below-knee prosthesis; the natural knee joint is preserved.
- **WHO (Wrist Hand Orthosis):** A brace stabilizing the wrist while allowing appropriate hand function.
