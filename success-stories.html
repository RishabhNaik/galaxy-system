Tip-Up Plate Doorway System

Capt. Shubhajeet's Independent Mobility

Mr. Shubhajeet, an Ex-Indian Army Captain, suffered a severe spinal cord injury in the line of duty due to a Bofors jeep toppling accident, leaving him wheelchair-bound.

To help him transfer in and out of his personal vehicle without relying on physical lifting from caregivers, Galaxy System installed a custom Tip-Up Plate transfer system at the driver's doorway. The fold-out bridge allows effortless sliding transfers directly from his wheelchair to the driver's seat.

"The Tip-Up plate mechanism gave me back my independence to travel whenever I choose."

Mr. Rajshekar's Annual Solo Expeditions

Mr. Rajshekar, affected by right-leg polio, refused to let physical limitations curtail his passion for travel.

With his vehicle customized by Galaxy System, he drives independently across India and embarks on solo highway road trips every single year with total peace of mind and safety.

"Every year I take on new long-distance road trips by myself. Galaxy System's modifications have never let me down."
Left Foot Accelerator Assembly

Shivaprasad's Pain-Free Driving

Due to severe right knee pain, Shivaprasad sir was finding it increasingly painful to operate standard vehicle pedals. He faced the prospect of hiring a chauffeur or giving up driving entirely.

Our workshop installed a customized, high-reliability left-foot accelerator system with a safety block over the original pedal. The setup was adjusted to match his specific comfort and movement range.
