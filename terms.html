2. Assessment & Vehicle Feasibility

All vehicle modifications (such as push-pull hand controls, left-foot accelerators, and electronic clutches) require a prior physical inspection of the vehicle and a consultation regarding the driver's posture and joint movement:

  • Modification feasibility is determined after workshop evaluation of the target vehicle model and condition.
  • Clients are responsible for ensuring their vehicle is in sound mechanical operating condition prior to installation.
  • Individual adaptations are engineered to meet safety standards and RTO guidelines in Karnataka.

3. Safety, RTO Approvals & Compliance

Galaxy System fabricates adaptive vehicle controls engineered for safety and durability. Assistance with RTO certificate endorsement is provided according to regional regulations. Drivers must comply with local licensing laws and drive only safety-certified modifications tailored to their driving status.

4. Warranty & After-Sales Maintenance

Custom mechanical modifications installed by Galaxy System carry our standard craftsmanship warranty against structural or manufacturing defects. Regular inspection at our Rajajinagar workshop is recommended to ensure ongoing control precision.
