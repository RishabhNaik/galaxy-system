About Galaxy System

Mobility Designed Around Independence

Galaxy System is a Bengaluru-based mobility solutions company focused on helping differently abled individuals move, drive, and travel with greater confidence, comfort, and independence.

We specialize in two core pillars of independent mobility: custom-fitted adaptive vehicle modifications (such as hand controls, left-foot accelerators, and electronic clutches) and advanced prosthetic and orthotic limbs designed for everyday active life.

Explore how standard vehicle controls are transformed into custom, safety-certified adaptive solutions.

Before
Standard Pedals

Driver relies entirely on lower limbs to operate throttle and brakes.

After Modification
Push-Pull Hand Control Lever

Hand Control System Installation

For individuals with lower-limb restrictions, we install a high-end push-pull hand controller. The mechanical assembly is anchored securely to the vehicle structure using bespoke, CAD-designed brackets.

  • • Safety-cleared mechanical design
  • • Hand throttle and braking levers with ergonomic grip
  • • Original pedals remain intact and functional for other drivers

Before
Standard Manual Clutch

Driver must press the heavy pedal down on every gear shift.

After Modification
Touch-Sensor Gear Knob

Pedal is bypassed by a motorized clutch actuator system.

Electronic Clutch Automation

Our electronic clutch automation system makes manual vehicles much easier to operate. A sensor embedded in the gear knob registers contact and engages the clutch automatically, removing pedal strain completely.

  • • Ideal for heavy bumper-to-bumper city traffic
  • • Bypasses manual clutch pedal operation
  • • Turn-on/turn-off switch to toggle back to standard manual mode

Before
Right Foot Pedal

Accelerator lies solely on the right side of the footwell.

After Modification
Left Foot Pedal Plate

Detachable pedal plate transfers control to the left side.

Detachable Left Foot Accelerator

Designed for drivers who have limited or painful right leg movement. We install a left-foot pedal linked mechanically to the throttle. The mechanism features a safety guard to prevent accidental right-side pedal pressing.

  • • High-strength mechanical transmission linkage
  • • Quick-release plate allows detaching pedal in 10 seconds
  • • Restores standard right-side setup for family members
