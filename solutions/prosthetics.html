Clinical Guidance

Prosthetics vs. Orthotics

Prosthetics: Artificial limbs designed to replace a missing body part following amputation or congenital limb difference.

Orthotics: Supportive medical devices designed to support, align, correct, or protect an existing body part affected by polio, stroke, cerebral palsy, or injury.

Custom Fabrication & Gait Training Period

Universal prosthetics cannot be bought "off-the-shelf." Every device must be individually custom-fabricated after clinical measurement, followed by a mandatory practice and gait training period to build strength and walk naturally.

Our Process

Patient-Centric Fitting & Gait Training

Getting a new prosthetic limb or orthotic support is a journey. At Galaxy System, we blend advanced biomedical manufacturing with personalized physical therapy to ensure you achieve seamless, pain-free mobility.

3D Anatomical Mapping

We use high-resolution 3D scanners to digitize limb shape, ensuring the customized socket distributes weight evenly without skin irritation.

Multi-Stage Trial Fittings

Before finalizing the design, you will test diagnostic check-sockets to verify comfort and alignment in motion.

Gait Training & Rehab

Our clinical physiotherapists guide you through posture alignment, balance, and natural walk pattern re-education.

Long-Term Adjustments

We provide lifetime socket adjustments and component servicing at our Bengaluru workshop.
