Adaptive Driving
Mobility Modifications

We custom-design, fabricate, and install RTO-approved controls for disabled drivers. Authorised by the Government of Karnataka to conduct vehicle accessibility modifications, our solutions respect original vehicle systems and are built around safety-first biomedical engineering.

Consultation First

Selecting the Right Vehicle Before Purchase

Two most common questions we hear are: "Can I drive a car?" and "Which car should I get?" We strongly recommend consulting Galaxy System before purchasing a vehicle. Recommending the right vehicle (transmission, cabin height, pedal spacing) beforehand significantly lowers modification costs and ensures safer, long-term ergonomics.

Common Pitfall to Avoid

Many individuals over-rely on advice from well-meaning family or friends when picking a vehicle or adaptive system. Because every physical disability, muscle strength profile, and joint movement range is unique, your modification must be individually assessed and custom-engineered for you.

Complete Adaptations

Custom Adaptive Solutions

Every device preserves original vehicle integrity while guaranteeing complete mechanical and operational safety.

01. Push-Pull Hand Control System

Designed for bilateral lower limb disability, post-polio paralysis, or spinal cord injury. Mechanically connects lever to foot pedals — pushing lever forward applies brakes, while pulling lever accelerates. Original foot pedals remain functional for other drivers.

Bilateral Lower Limb Support
Left Foot Accelerator Quick-Release Mechanism & Guard Plate

02. Left Foot Accelerator

Installed to the left side of the brake pedal for drivers with right lower limb limitations (polio, amputation, weakness). Operates accelerator and brake using natural left leg movement. Features a quick-release detachable plate and safety guard to shield original pedal.

Right Leg Limitation Support
Ring Accelerator System Under/Over Steering Ring Controls

03. Ring Accelerator & Brake Lever

Advanced driving controls engineered for automatic vehicles. Driver accelerates by pressing a ring mounted under/over steering wheel while operating a separate hand brake lever. Allows continuous two-handed steering stability and reduces long-distance driver fatigue.

Automatic Vehicle Speciality
Automatic Clutch Gear Knob Sensor & Motorized Actuator

04. Automatic Clutch System

Enables drivers with left-leg disabilities to drive manual transmission vehicles without depressing the clutch pedal. Sensor on gear knob triggers automatic clutch engagement, allowing manual gear shifts without physical foot effort.

Manual Transmission Adaptation
Steering Keypad Module Wireless Secondary Functions Keypad

05. Steering Wheel Transmitter

Consolidates secondary controls (horn, indicators, headlights, high beam, wipers, hazard lights) directly on a steering wheel keypad module. Allows single upper-limb drivers to operate all functions without taking hand off wheel.

Single Arm Functionality
Person Lift System Motorized Wheelchair Transfer Hoist

06. Person Lift System

Motorized hoist or swivel lift mechanism aiding wheelchair users entering and exiting cabin. Significantly reduces physical lifting strain on caregivers and restores dignity during vehicle transfers.

Wheelchair Transfer Lift
Tip-Up Transfer Plate Fold-Out Doorway Sliding Bridge

07. Tip-Up Transfer Plate

Platform-based transfer bridge fitted alongside door threshold. Folds out to bridge gap between wheelchair seat and vehicle cushion for effortless sliding transfers. Popular solution chosen for ex-defence personnel and spinal cord injury drivers.

Doorway Transfer Bridge
Pedal Extensions Extended Mechanical Assembly & Riser

08. Pedal Extensions

Custom mechanical pedal extensions designed for drivers of short stature or dwarfism. Brings accelerator, brake, and clutch pedals closer to seating position while maintaining proper leverage and emergency force.

Short Stature Adaptations

Safety & Approvals

RTO Approved & Certified Modifications

Under standard Indian vehicle guidelines, any driving modification must comply with ARAI safety codes and obtain registration approval. As an authorised retrofitter by the Government of Karnataka, Galaxy System guides you through the entire process.

Invalid Carriage Update

We issue the required Form 22 and fabrication certificates to register your modified car as an "Invalid Carriage" at your local RTO.

No Chassis Cutting

All modifications are mounted using structural brackets anchoring to factory points, preserving original crash safety zones.

ARAI Compliant Links

Every push-pull and clutch system uses industrial-certified rods, cables, and bearings to avoid linkage failures.

Road Tax Exemption

Our certification documents help you apply for applicable road tax exemptions and insurance concessions for disabled drivers.
