# FAQ Section

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can adaptive driving systems be installed in any car?

Solutions depend on the vehicle model and the user’s individual requirements. Our team provides assessment-based recommendations.

### Is training required after installing hand controls?

Many users may require familiarization and practice to comfortably adapt to new driving controls.

### What is an electronic clutch system?

An electronic clutch system assists with clutch operation, reducing manual effort during driving.

### Are modifications customized?

Yes. Every adaptive solution is designed around the user’s mobility needs, comfort, and driving requirements.

### Do you provide consultation before modification?

Yes. Understanding the user’s requirements is an important part of the process.
